A thumbs-down starts an investigation, not a diagnosis. The workflow replays the question, collects evidence, separates a content gap from a configuration issue, and routes to an owner; routing is ordinary code, the model only proposes the likely cause. Keep the human jobs explicit: approving a reference answer is not the same job as owning an incident, and doubtful cases go to a person rather than turning confidence into authority.

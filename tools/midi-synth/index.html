MIDI synth

How to use

Play a note. Shape the sound. Keep playing.

Ready to play

Wave shape sets the starting tone.

Amplitude envelope Level over time

Rise, settle, hold, then fade after key-up.

s

How fast each note fades in.

s

How fast the note settles after the attack.

How loud the note stays while held.

s

How long each note rings after you let go.

Movement (LFO) Slow wave

A slow wave that moves one control for you. Depth at zero is off.

Hz

How fast the movement repeats.

How strong the movement is. Zero keeps the sound still.

LFO is off while Depth is 0. Raise Depth to hear the movement.

Low-pass filter Sound colour

Lower cutoff softens the sound. Resonance adds an edge.

Hz

Lower is mellower, higher is brighter.

Emphasis around the filter edge: the wah.

HeldNone

Press a key, click or tap to play.

Held = key down. Pedal = sustained by MIDI pedal. Releasing = scheduled fade window, not a per-note loudness reading.

Preview on: a tweak plays one full note with its starting sound, and the next tweak waits until it ends, so sounds are easy to compare. Play test note repeats it; Esc stops all sound.

Drag dials · Shift for fine changes · Type exact values
Connect a MIDI keyboard Optional

Connect a MIDI keyboard to play with it.

Note velocity and the sustain pedal are honoured. MIDI input stays on your device; no sound or key presses are uploaded.

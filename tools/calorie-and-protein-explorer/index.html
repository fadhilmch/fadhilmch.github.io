Sources and limits

Resting rate: Mifflin-St Jeor (1990), the equation dietetic guidelines rate best for the general adult population; individual variation is easily ±10%. Maintenance adds a standard activity multiplier. Protein: WHO (Technical Report 935) and EFSA both set the adult reference at 0,83 g/kg/day; the ISSN position stand gives 1,4-2,0 g/kg/day as sufficient for most exercising people building or keeping muscle; the Morton et al. (2018) meta-analysis puts the benefit ceiling near 1,6 g/kg/day with an upper confidence bound of 2,2 g/kg/day. Not for pregnancy, kidney disease, eating-disorder recovery or under-18s - those need a clinician, not a slider.

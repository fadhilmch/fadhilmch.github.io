    A starting point, not a guarantee of soft or golden light. Clouds, trees, buildings and hills can change the result.

    Shade backup & before-you-go checklist

    If the timing doesn't work: open shade

    On a sunny day, find open shade and an uncluttered background. Avoid patches of sunlight on the face. You don't have to wait for golden hour.

    Before you go

    • Check cloud, rain and wind. No forecast is included.
    • Scout the spot and a shade backup. Buildings and terrain are not modelled.
    • Check squinting, uneven shadows and face exposure.
    How these windows are chosen

    SunCalc 2.1.1, apparent sun-centre altitude 0–6°, sampled each minute across the selected local day. Boundaries are approximate to a minute; they are not exactly sunrise or sunset. Windows may be long or absent at high latitudes. No quality score or universal best time is claimed.

    City search uses a bundled GeoNames snapshot, not an external API or your GPS. City-centre coordinates are approximate; use exact coordinates and the matching IANA time zone for a specific shoot. Daylight saving is included. Avoid ambiguous repeated/skipped local hours for exact timing.

    Planning limits & light definitions

    SunCalc 2.1.1, degrees clockwise from true north. "Glow" uses SunCalc's +6° crossing; its window runs between sunrise and that morning crossing, or the evening crossing and sunset. The chart marks 0-6°. Golden light is not guaranteed: clouds, terrain, buildings and atmospheric conditions matter. No weather or geolocation service is used. The map loads OpenStreetMap tiles. City search loads a same-site data file.

    Custom locations require a matching IANA timezone. Avoid the repeated or skipped hour on DST-change dates for exact shoot timing.

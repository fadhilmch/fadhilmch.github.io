Reading the numbers, assumptions and guardrails
  • 15 calendar days, about 2.1 weeks. Rounding up to 3 full weeks balances the weekday mix, but that is calendar planning, not a stopping rule.
  • Detecting a move from 10,0% to 11,0%: +1,0 percentage points, 10% relative. These are not the same unit.
  • Days come from traffic alone. Decide the stopping rule before you start; peeking daily and stopping when it looks good inflates false positives.
  • 50/50 split between the two variants.
  • Two-sided significance level α = 0.05, power 80%.
  • Normal approximation to two independent proportions.
  • One primary metric, decided before launch.

The maths behind this tool: p-values, built up from coin flips and the A/B testing note.

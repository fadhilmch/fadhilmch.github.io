Co-Ferment Blueberry Coffee - High Clarity AeroPress

Brew

0:00 of 1:55Step 2
  1. Grind & prep: Set Silberthal to scale 4-4.5. Grind 13 grams co-ferment blueberry coffee (light-medium roast) fresh. Stack two paper filters in the AeroPress cap, rinse both with hot water over your mug, then discard the rinse water. Assemble AeroPress upright on your mug.
  2. 0:00 - Bloom pour: Start your timer. Pour 35g of 250 grams brew water (~90-92°C) over the grounds. Swirl once gently. Let bloom - do not touch until 0:30.
  3. 0:30 - Main pour: Pour the remaining water steadily in a spiral until you reach the full 250 grams brew water (~90-92°C). Finish pouring by 0:55. No stirring - zero agitation from here.
  4. 0:55 - Steep: Set down and wait undisturbed until 1:15.
  5. 1:15 - Begin pressing: Insert the plunger and press as slowly and steadily as possible. Target: finish pressing at 1:55. Stop immediately at the first hiss - do not push through it.
  6. 1:55 - Done: Remove AeroPress and serve immediately. The cup should feel light and tea-like, with blueberry and fermentation notes sitting cleanly on top - transparent and sweet, not heavy or syrupy.

Notes

Tuned for maximum clarity and delicacy. Coarser grind, shorter steep, double filter - less body, more transparency in the blueberry and fermentation notes.

Dial-in guide

  • Too heavy / syrupy: go one click coarser on Silberthal.
  • Too thin / sour: add 1g coffee or extend steep to 1:30 before pressing.

<h2>Tips for the best result</h2>
<ul>
<li>Crispier crust: bake uncovered until deeply golden brown.</li>
<li>Airier crumb: don’t overproof or aggressively degas during shaping.</li>
<li>More sour flavor: extend the cold fermentation to 16-20 hours.</li>
<li>Bosch Universal: use the dough hook on low speed and avoid excessive kneading.</li>
<li>Best starting point: follow this recipe with a 12-hour cold proof. It should produce a rustic loaf with a crisp crust, chewy interior and pleasantly tangy flavor.</li>

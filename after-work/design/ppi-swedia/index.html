PPI Swedia

PPI Swedia is the Indonesian student association in Sweden - a community and communication forum for Indonesian students across the country. This page is about its logo: the symbols inside it, the colours, and where you see it.

The primary lockup: Garuda crest with the two-line wordmark, on the brand navy.

The ideaTwo countries, one crest - taken apart.

1
Three Crowns

Sweden's national emblem - the country where the members live and study.

2
Garuda

The mythical bird of Indonesia, also part of the national emblem Garuda Pancasila. It stands for Indonesian students with high determination, pride in their nation, and the will to uphold Pancasila as the national principle.

3
Shield

PPI Swedia as a family that takes care of, protects, supports and embraces its members, so everyone can study well and graduate.

4
Book

Knowledge and intelligence - a place to learn, discuss, exchange ideas, share knowledge and grow.

5
Two flags

The Indonesian and Swedish flags side by side in the book: students who learn and combine the education, knowledge and culture of both countries, hoping to bring the best of both home to Indonesia.

ColoursSwedish blue and yellow meet Indonesian red, on a deep navy.

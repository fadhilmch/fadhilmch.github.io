---
title: Rustic Sourdough Bread - Bosch Universal
cuisine: Bread
serves: 1 loaf (~1 kg)
time: 2 days, overnight cold proof
tags: [Bread]
ingredients:
  - "450 g Vetemjöl Special"
  - "50 g rågsikt"
  - "350 g water, room temperature (330 g for the autolyse, 20 g held back)"
  - "100 g active sourdough starter (100% hydration)"
  - "10 g salt"
steps:
  - "Day 1 - Activate starter: feed the starter at a 1:2:2 ratio and wait until doubled, bubbly and near peak, about 4-8 hours at 24°C."
  - "Autolyse (30-45 min): add the Vetemjöl Special, rågsikt and 330 g water to the Bosch Universal. Mix with the dough hook on low for 1-2 minutes. Cover and rest."
  - "Mix and knead (7-12 min): add the starter and mix on low for 2-3 minutes. Add the salt and the remaining 20 g water. Knead on low for 5-8 minutes until smooth, elastic and slightly sticky. Avoid overheating the dough."
  - "Bulk fermentation (about 4-7 hours at 23-24°C): transfer to a container. Do 1-2 stretch-and-fold sets during the first 90 minutes if needed. Ready when the dough has increased 40-60%, shows bubbles and jiggles slightly."
  - "Shape (20-30 min): turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Pre-shape into a round ball, rest 20 minutes, then shape tightly into a boule."
  - "Cold proof (8-16 hours): place seam-side up in a floured banneton or towel-lined bowl. Cover and refrigerate at about 4°C."
  - "Day 2 - Preheat: heat the oven and Dutch oven to 250°C for 40-45 minutes."
  - "Score: remove the dough straight from the fridge, turn onto parchment paper and score about 1 cm deep."
  - "Bake: 20 minutes covered at 250°C, then 20-25 minutes uncovered at 220°C."
  - "Cool on a rack at room temperature for 2 hours before slicing."
notes: "73% hydration. Bulk fermentation begins when the starter is mixed into the dough. At a ~23.8°C room temperature, focus on the 40-60% rise rather than a fixed fermentation time."

## Tips for the best result

- Crispier crust: bake uncovered until deeply golden brown.
- Airier crumb: don't overproof or aggressively degas during shaping.
- More sour flavor: extend the cold fermentation to 16-20 hours.
- Bosch Universal: use the dough hook on low speed and avoid excessive kneading.
- Best starting point: follow this recipe with a 12-hour cold proof. It should produce a rustic loaf with a crisp crust, chewy interior and pleasantly tangy flavor.

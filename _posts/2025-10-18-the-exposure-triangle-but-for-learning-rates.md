summary: Aperture, shutter and ISO behave a lot like learning rate, batch size and

---

Photography exposure is a useful reminder that a system of controls is coupled. Aperture, shutter speed and ISO share a brightness budget; changing one changes the pressure on the others.

Learning rate, batch size and warmup are also easier to reason about as a configuration than as isolated magic numbers. The analogy is imperfect, but it encourages deliberate trade-offs and careful observation rather than tuning one control blindly.

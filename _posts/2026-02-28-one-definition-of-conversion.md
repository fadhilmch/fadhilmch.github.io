When teams define conversion differently, a dashboard comparison becomes a debate about SQL instead of a discussion about the product. A metric semantic layer gives experiments and reporting a shared definition to reuse.

The hard part is not only building a catalog. It is agreeing on ownership, documenting meaning and making the governed definition easier to use than a local copy. Reuse is what turns a schema into a working practice.

21:T631,<p>Decide what kind of follow-up a message is before choosing its subject. Checked in this order:</p>
<ol>
<li>Self-contained question, even right after a clarifying question: keep it as typed. Copy no terms from earlier turns.</li>
<li>Fragment answering a clarifying question: pending subject plus the new detail.</li>
<li>Bare topic after an ordinary answer: new query.</li>
<li>Complaint that the answer missed (“really nothing on it? who qualifies for it?”): pronouns stay on the user’s original subject, not the bot’s alternative.</li>
<li>Neutral pronoun follow-up: the item the bot explained, not one it only mentioned.</li>
<li>“Yes” or “no” to an offered option: apply it or drop it.</li>
<li>Greeting, thanks, goodbye: unchanged.</li>
</ol>
<p>Location: inherit an earlier one only for a related follow-up. An explicit location wins. Never infer a location from the language of the message.</p>
<p>Gotchas: “How do I apply?” is a full sentence that still needs the subject. Resolve pronouns with the whole message, not recency. Two replies to the same bot message can need opposite rules, so test the pairs (<a href="/notes/prompt-testing/">[[prompt-testing]]</a>). Write it as an ordered list (<a href="/notes/decision-list-prompts/">[[decision-list-prompts]]</a>), not cases with exceptions.

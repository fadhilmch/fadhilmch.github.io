20:T768,<p>Model APIs are stateless: every request carries the history you choose to send. So the history is your data, not the framework’s (<a href="/notes/agent-state/">[[agent-state]]</a>).</p>
<p>Decision rules:</p>
<ul>
<li>Save each turn from values you own: the user’s message and the final answer your orchestrator returns. Never read it back from an agent framework’s internal model context. Its timing can leave the answer out, and then your saved turns are silently empty.</li>
<li>Store whole user/assistant pairs as plain strings, plus a schema version (<a href="/notes/state-versioning/">[[state-versioning]]</a>) and counters, in a <a href="/notes/session-store/">[[session-store]]</a>.</li>
<li>Bound it twice: what you store (we keep 8 turns) and what each step reads (the rewriter reads the last 10 messages).</li>
<li>Give the store a TTL so idle sessions expire.</li>
<li>Validate on load. Unknown version or a malformed turn means start fresh, with the reason on the trace.</li>
<li>Pass history into the step that needs it as an argument. Here that is the rewrite in <a href="/notes/query-rewriting/">[[query-rewriting]]</a>; search and answer get the rewritten query.</li>
</ul>
<pre><code class="hljs language-python">state[<span class="hljs-string">"turns"</span>] = [*state[<span class="hljs-string">"turns"</span>], {<span class="hljs-string">"user"</span>: q, <span class="hljs-string">"assistant"</span>: answer}][-<span class="hljs-number">8</span>:]
</code></pre>

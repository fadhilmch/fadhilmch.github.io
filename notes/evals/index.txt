1e:T4e4,<p>A fluent answer proves nothing, so evaluate the loop rather than the vibe. Compare relevance, retrieval recall, groundedness and latency against a baseline, and read examples next to the scores: grounded, useful and found-the-right-document are three different failures. Offline golden sets give controlled comparisons; production feedback shows what the set missed, and a thumbs-down needs investigation before it means anything. My workflow produces a report for engineers rather than an automatic gate - a choice, not a rule.</p>
<p>When a score drops, the fix depends on which failure it is:</p>
